# SpendWise

SpendWise tracks shared expenses and repayments within groups. These terms reflect the agreed MVP user journeys.

## Language

**Profile**:
A person's SpendWise display identity, shared across their group memberships and sign-in sessions.

**Group**:
A collection of members who track shared expenses and repayments in one currency.

**Membership**:
A user's participation in a group, which can continue historically after the user leaves or is removed.

**Active member**:
A group member who currently has access to the group and can manage its membership and financial entries.

**Inactive group**:
A group whose final active member has departed after all direct balances were settled. Its historical records remain, but its invitation links can no longer be used to join.

**Expense**:
A shared cost paid by one member and allocated among participating members. The payer does not have to participate in the allocation.

**Expense share**:
The portion of an expense allocated to one participating member.
_Avoid_: Split when referring to an individual member's allocated amount.

**Split method**:
The rule used to allocate an expense among participants: equal shares, exact amounts, or percentages.

**Repayment**:
A recorded payment made outside SpendWise from one group member to another to reduce debt. It is not spending.
_Avoid_: Money transfer when referring to an action performed by SpendWise.

**Direct balance**:
The remaining amount owed between two members within one group after offsetting their mutual expenses and repayments. Debts are never rerouted through other members.

**Invitation link**:
A shareable invitation that a signed-in, eligible user explicitly accepts to join a group.

**Financial entry**:
An expense or a recorded repayment within a group.

**Change history**:
A record of corrections to financial entries identifying who made each change, when it happened, and the previous values.
